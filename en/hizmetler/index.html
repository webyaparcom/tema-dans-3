Questions

Before you step on the floor

Turn the deck with the arrows or by swiping.

  1. 1 / 6person_add

    Do I need to bring a partner?

    No. In partner classes partners rotate every few minutes; most people come on their own.

  2. 2 / 6checkroom

    What should I wear?

    Comfortable clothes and clean trainers not worn outdoors are enough; slippery-soled shoes for tango can come later.

  3. 3 / 6confirmation_number

    Is the trial class really free?

    Yes; you can join each beginner group once for free. Your spot is confirmed with a call.

  4. 4 / 6stairs

    How do I move up a level?

    At the end of the term you have a short talk with your instructor; if you do not feel ready, you can stay at the same level for another term.

  5. 5 / 6groups

    How big are the groups?

    At most 14 people. In partner classes we try to balance leads and follows.

  6. 6 / 6favorite

    Are there private lessons for a wedding dance?

    Yes. A short routine is set to the couple’s song; we plan the date and number of lessons together.
